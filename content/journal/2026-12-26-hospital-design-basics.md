Healthcare buildings must work under pressure, every hour of every day. Designing **Alpha Multispeciality Hospital** reinforced a few principles we apply to every clinic and hospital.

## 1. Separate the flows
Emergency, outpatient, inpatient, staff, supplies and waste should not cross unnecessarily. At Alpha, a clearly expressed ground floor **separates the main and emergency entrances** from the street.

## 2. Stack by intensity
Public reception and outpatient areas sit at the base where footfall is highest; wards and treatment are above, quieter and more controlled.

## 3. Make it legible
A generous **reception and circulation spine** helps anxious patients and visitors find their way without asking.

## 4. Light heals
Daylight and views improve patient wellbeing and staff alertness. Alpha's façade uses angled timber-toned louvres to **shade ward windows** while keeping them bright.

## 5. Hygiene by design
Clean, washable, seamless surfaces; coved skirting; easy-clean ceilings; and well-planned storage keep infection control practical.

## 6. Plan for change
Medical technology changes fast. Regular structural grids and accessible service routes make future upgrades easier.
