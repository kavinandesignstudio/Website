The best house design starts with the right plot. Before you sign, walk the site with these ten questions — ideally with your architect.

## 1. Road width and access
Road width affects permissible floor area, setbacks and how easily trucks and materials can reach the site.

## 2. Plot shape and dimensions
Rectangular plots are easiest to plan. Irregular plots can work beautifully (Strata in Yercaud sits on a very irregular site), but need design thinking early.

## 3. Orientation
Note where north is and which side faces the road. It affects light, heat, ventilation — and Vastu, if that matters to you.

## 4. Levels and drainage
Is the plot lower than the road? Does water collect in the monsoon? Low plots need filling and good drainage planning.

## 5. Soil
Black cotton soil, filled ground or rock all change the foundation design and cost. A simple soil test is money well spent.

## 6. Water
Check groundwater depth and quality, and whether a municipal supply is available.

## 7. Neighbours and views
What is built — or could be built — around you? Plan windows and open spaces accordingly.

## 8. Trees and features
Existing trees are precious. Design around them where possible.

## 9. Documents
Check title, patta, approved layout and encumbrance with a qualified lawyer before purchase.

## 10. Rules
Local building rules decide setbacks, height and coverage. Your architect can tell you roughly what you can build before you buy.
