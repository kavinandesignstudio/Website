Building approval can feel confusing. Here's a simple overview of what is typically involved for a residential building in Tamil Nadu. Rules and authorities vary (CMDA in the Chennai metropolitan area, DTCP and local bodies elsewhere), so always confirm current requirements for your site.

## Documents you'll usually be asked for

- Title / sale deed and patta
- Approved layout copy (for plots in layouts)
- Encumbrance certificate
- Latest property tax receipt (for existing buildings)
- Identity and address proof of the owner
- Structural stability certificate (where required)

## Drawings your architect prepares

- Site plan showing setbacks, road width and access
- Floor plans, elevations and sections
- Area statement (FSI, coverage, parking)
- Service plans as required — rainwater harvesting, septic tank/sewer connection

## Typical sequence

1. **Feasibility** — what can be built under current rules
2. **Design** — approved by you
3. **Approval drawings** — prepared to the rules
4. **Submission** — usually through the online portal
5. **Scrutiny & site inspection**
6. **Approval / permit** — then construction can begin

## Tips

- Don't start construction before approval.
- Build as per the approved plan; deviations can cause problems later with completion certificates, loans and resale.
- Keep copies of every approved drawing.
