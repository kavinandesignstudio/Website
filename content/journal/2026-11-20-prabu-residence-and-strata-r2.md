Two projects moved forward on our drawing boards this month, one rooted in Kumbakonam's heritage and one perched on the slopes of Yercaud.

## Mr. Prabu Residence, Kumbakonam

At the heart of this home is a **mutram**, the open-to-sky courtyard that has organised Tamil houses for centuries. Turned timber pillars and a carved cornice frame a sunken court, with a stone planter at its centre catching the daylight.

The palette comes from the region:

- Patterned floor tiles and a deep green dado
- Warm ivory walls and dark timber doors with brass detailing
- A red arched niche carrying a hand-drawn **kolam** motif
- Jaali windows and high clerestories for light and cross-ventilation

The living room pairs this character with modern comfort: a dark coffered timber ceiling, soft seating and a slim media wall suspended on brass chains.

> A courtyard is more than a space. It is how a house breathes.

## Strata, Yercaud: the R2 design

Our boutique hill hotel for Strata Luxury Stay has evolved. The latest revision keeps the eight named suites and the stacked, stepping massing, and sharpens the identity with:

- **Timber jaali screens** that filter light and give the suites privacy
- Slender sloping roof canopies over the upper floors
- Planted terraces and balconies at every level
- A crisp white palette that stands out against the forest
