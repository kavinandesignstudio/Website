Yercaud in the Shevaroy Hills is cool, misty and green — and steep. For our client Mr. Shankar of Kumbakonam, the brief was a boutique hill hotel on a compact plot of **4,287 sq.ft** that would feel generous, private and deeply connected to the forest.

## Like layers of rock

The idea came from the hillside itself. Instead of one block, **Strata** stacks a series of offset volumes up the slope, like layers of rock. Each level steps back and cantilevers forward so that every suite gets its own view, its own light and its own balcony.

## Working with the slope, not against it

On a steep site, the cheapest square foot is the one you don't excavate. Strata places **parking on stilts** at road level, keeping cutting and retaining to a minimum. Guests arrive at a reception lounge on the first floor and move up through the building towards the best views.

## Eight suites, eight names

The hotel has eight individually named rooms:

- **Strata Nests** — Moss, Pine and Ridge
- **Strata Vistas** — Mist, Fern, Canopy and Brook
- **Summit Strata Signature** — the top-floor suite

The whole building culminates in a **dining hall and kitchens on the top floor** with panoramic views over the valley.

## Designing for a hill climate

Hill architecture is about comfort in cool, damp weather:

1. **Pitched timber roofs** shed heavy rain and add warmth.
2. **Deep balconies** protect glazing from driving rain and give guests outdoor rooms.
3. **Curved bay windows** capture the view — and the low winter sun.
4. **Compact suites** are easier to keep warm and cosy.
