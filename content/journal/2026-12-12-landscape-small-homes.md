You don't need an estate to have a garden. Some of the most beautiful landscapes we design are only a few square metres.

## The front garden
The setback in front of your house is your first impression. A layered planting bed, a tree for shade, a permeable paved path and soft up-lighting can transform the street elevation — as at Mr. RK's Residence in Dindigul.

## The courtyard
An internal court brings light, air and greenery into the heart of the home. Use shade-tolerant plants, pebbles or a small water feature, and plan drainage carefully.

## The terrace garden
Roofs take the most heat. A terrace garden cools the floor below and creates a new outdoor room. It needs proper waterproofing, root barriers, drainage and lightweight soil mix — plan it with your architect, not as an afterthought.

## Choose the right plants
- **Native and climate-adapted** species need less water
- **Layer** trees, shrubs and groundcover
- Use **fragrant** plants near seating and windows
- Avoid aggressive roots near foundations and tanks

## Light it softly
Warm, low-glare lighting on trees and paths makes gardens usable in the evening — when Tamil Nadu is at its most pleasant outdoors.
