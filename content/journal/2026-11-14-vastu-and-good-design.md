Many of our clients ask for Vastu-compliant homes. We see no conflict between Vastu and good architecture — many of its principles align closely with climate-responsive design.

## Where Vastu and climate agree

- **Open north and east** — gentle morning light and good ventilation
- **Heavier, closed south and west** — protection from harsh afternoon sun
- **A light, open centre** — echoing the traditional courtyard and its cooling stack effect
- **Kitchen in the south-east** — morning light, prevailing breezes carrying smoke away

## How we work with Vastu

1. **Ask early.** We note your Vastu priorities in the brief — which are essential, which are preferences.
2. **Plan, then refine.** We plan for light, air and flow first, then align room positions with your priorities.
3. **Be honest about trade-offs.** On tight or irregular plots, not every rule can be met; we show options and let you decide.
4. **Consult together.** If you work with a Vastu consultant, we are happy to review plans with them.

## What we won't compromise

Structural safety, fire safety, building rules, natural light and ventilation. A good home must first be safe, healthy and comfortable.

> A plan can be Vastu-aware and still be beautiful, bright and practical.
