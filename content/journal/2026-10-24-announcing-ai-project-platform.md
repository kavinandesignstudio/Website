Every project we design involves many people: the client and their family, structural and MEP consultants, contractors, vendors and our own team. Good buildings depend on all of them working from the **same, current information**. Too often, they don't.

That's why we are building an **AI-based Project Management and Design Management platform** for our clients and their project teams.

## The problem we want to solve

Most problems on site don't start with bad design. They start with a lost message:

- A superseded drawing gets built because nobody knew a new revision existed
- An approval given on a phone call is remembered differently a month later
- A client living abroad can't see what's pending or what has been paid for
- A consultant's comment never reaches the contractor

## One shared source of truth

The platform brings everything into one place: the **drawing register** with every revision, **approvals and decisions**, the **schedule**, **site photos and reports**, **RFIs**, and **budget and payments** — each person seeing exactly what they need.

> Our architects stay in charge of every decision. AI does the paperwork — summarising, reminding and flagging.

## Useful for every kind of client

- **Design only** — the latest approved drawings, revisions and sign-offs, so your own contractor always builds from the right sheet.
- **Design + Supervision** — plus site visit reports, quality checklists, RFIs and snag lists.
- **Design + Build** — plus the live construction schedule, daily progress photos, BOQ and stage-wise payments.

## Where AI helps

- **Plain-language weekly summaries** — what moved, what's pending, what needs your decision
- **Meeting minutes and action items** — written up and followed up automatically
- **Early warnings** — when approvals, deliveries or work stages start to slip

## What happens next

The platform is in development. We will pilot it first on our ongoing projects, then open early access to clients, consultants and contractors.
