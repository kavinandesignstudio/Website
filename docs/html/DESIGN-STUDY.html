Design Study — Kavinan Design Studio Website

The research, decisions and system behind the site.

1. Brief, distilled

A high-quality, professional website for an architecture, interior, landscape, project-management and design + build firm. Modern, contemporary, traditional and minimal. Multi-page. Light and dark mode. Practical FAQs, a journal that keeps itself fresh, strong SEO. Use Strata and S-Gan Villa as the hero imagery.

3. Benchmarks & design principles

Award-recognised architecture-practice sites (the kind featured on Awwwards, CSS Design Awards and in the portfolios of practices such as Studio Mumbai, Snøhetta, Herzog & de Meuron, Zaha Hadid Architects, Bjarke Ingels Group) share a consistent grammar:

  1. Imagery leads; the interface recedes. Full-bleed photography, generous white space, few colours.
  2. Editorial typography. A refined serif for headlines, a quiet sans for reading, small letter-spaced labels.
  3. A grid you can feel. Asymmetric 12-column layouts, aligned captions, consistent rhythm.
  4. Slow, deliberate motion. Fades, image reveals and gentle zooms — never bouncing widgets.
  5. Projects as stories. Facts, narrative, then galleries — not just thumbnails.

Our principles for Kavinan, derived from the brand line Rooted with design:

  • Rooted → warm paper tones, earthy terracotta and forest green from the logo, references to local craft

(jaali, clay tile, courtyard) in content.

  • Contemporary + traditional → a geometric display face (Outfit) that echoes the logo's bold

wordmark, paired with a calm, highly readable text face (DM Sans); tradition comes through in the imagery, materials and content rather than in ornament.

  • Minimal → one accent colour at a time, hairline rules instead of boxes, no stock photos, no clutter.
  • Built well → every page leads to proof (projects) and to action (WhatsApp / consultation).

Dark mode is deliberately not black: it uses a deep forest green drawn from the logo, so the site feels like the brand in both modes. Every text colour passes WCAG AA (4.5:1 or better).

Typography

  • Display: Outfit 200–500 — a clean geometric sans echoing the bold "KAVINAN" wordmark;

large headlines in light weights, accent words in orange at medium weight.

  • Text: DM Sans 400–700 — 15.5–17px fluid body, 1.7 line-height.
  • Labels: DM Sans 600, 0.72rem, 0.24em letter-spacing, uppercase — echoing the portfolio's spaced titles.

Layout & components

12-column grid, fluid gutters (16–56px), max width 1440px. Components: hero slideshow with Ken Burns, numbered section heads, statement paragraphs, stats with count-up, service rows with image peek, project cards (wide/tall pairs share heights), facts tables, transformation triptych, masonry galleries with lightbox, area-schedule table, process timeline, rotating quotes, FAQ accordions, editorial journal layout with sticky table of contents, CTA band, large-type footer.

Motion

Content fades up 28px; images unmask with a slow clip-path reveal; hero slides cross-fade over 1.6s. All motion is disabled for visitors who prefer reduced motion.

6. Content strategy

  • Voice: warm, calm, precise — an architect explaining clearly. British/Indian spelling.
  • Proof first: every service page lists real projects; every project page links its services.
  • Practical FAQs (31) grouped by the client's journey: getting started → fees → process →

construction → interiors → landscape → approvals → working remotely.

  • Journal: 12 articles written (6 live, 6 scheduled fortnightly to Jan 2027), mixing project

stories and homeowner guides that match real search questions.

  • Automation: studio updates → Claude writes an article + FAQs daily; a topic queue supplies a

weekly evergreen guide. All posts are rooted in real facts supplied by the studio.

  • Architectural quotes: Le Corbusier, Louis Kahn, Mies van der Rohe, Frank Lloyd Wright,

Charles Eames, Churchill — rotating on dark bands.

7. Performance, accessibility, security

AreaDecision
StackStatic HTML/CSS/JS generated by a dependency-free Python script — nothing to patch, nothing to hack, near-zero hosting cost
Images1.2 GB of source files → ~31 MB of WebP in three widths; srcset + sizes, lazy loading, explicit dimensions
JSOne small file, no frameworks, deferred
FontsTwo families, display=swap, preconnected
AccessibilitySemantic landmarks, skip link, focus styles, keyboard lightbox/menu, aria on toggles, alt text on every image, colour contrast checked on text tokens, reduced-motion support
Security headersX-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, X-Frame-Options, Permissions-Policy via Netlify _headers
PrivacyNo tracking unless a GA4 ID is added; privacy page included; form honeypot against spam
